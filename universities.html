Student Advisory

Tips for Renting a Hostel in Ghana

Key factors Ghanaian university students should consider before paying booking fees.

⚡

Standby Generators

With academic revision and online exam submissions, ask if the hostel has an automatic industrial generator that powers individual rooms or just corridor lights during power cuts (dumsor).

🚰

Water Security (Polytanks)

Municipal water supply can be erratic in student zones like Ayeduase (Kumasi) and Amamoma (Cape Coast). Confirm the hostel has overhead borehole tanks and pumping machines.

🛡️

Security & Curfew Rules

Check if the hostel provides 24/7 security porters, CCTV cameras, razor wire, and electric fencing, especially if you return late from Balme or KNUST library study sessions.
